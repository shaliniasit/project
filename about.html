Welcome To Our Hotel


Welcome to Elite Stay Grand Hotel,Prayagraj-Where comfort meets Luxury. Located in the Heart of the city at Civil Lines we are just 2 KM from Prayagraj junction and 5 KM from Triveni Sangam. We are the perfect choice for tourists, business travelers and families.

Combining supreme comfort, exquisite dining, and eco-friendly practices, we offer a tranquil retreat in the heart of the city. Inspired by traditional warmth and driven by world-class standards, our goal is to make every stay safe, inspiring, and truly memorable.

Mission & Vision


At Elite Stay Grand Hotel, our mission is to deliver an unparalleled hospitality experience by combining world-class luxury, supreme comfort, and personalized care for every guest. Rooted in the timeless tradition of "Atithi Devo Bhava" (Guest is God), we welcome every visitor with heartfelt warmth while offering premium accommodations, fine dining, and eco-friendly practices.

Looking ahead, our vision is to establish Elite Stay Grand Hotel as a globally recognized benchmark in hospitality by seamlessly blending cultural heritage with modern excellence—making us the premier destination of choice for families, business travelers, and international guests worldwide.
